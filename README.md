# 🛡️ Kenny Safety Nets

A modern, responsive, and SEO-optimized business website for **Kenny Safety Nets**, providing professional safety net installation services across Bangalore.

🌐 **Live Website:** https://www.kennysafetynets.in/

---

## 📌 About

Kenny Safety Nets offers reliable safety solutions for homes, apartments, villas, commercial buildings, schools, and industrial properties across Bangalore. This website is designed to provide customers with detailed information about services, service areas, and easy contact options.
